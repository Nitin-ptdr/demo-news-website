Couple caught in Capitol

Published September 26, 2025

At Capitol, where every student is busy with studies, a couple was seen acting like the world belongs to just them. Their public displays of love made all single students sigh loudly, wishing they were in their shoes - or maybe not! One student said, "If there was a contest for annoying couples, they would win easily." Many singles joked that couples should be banned from campus forever – because honestly, we get enough stress from exams without added love drama in every corner.
